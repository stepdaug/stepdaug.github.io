Realistic Synthetic Clinical Data Generation

I'm developing a novel engine to automate the generation of large amounts of realistically messy synthetic patient data. This creates a safe 'sandbox' to strategically probe for failures in atypical 'edge cases' where AI tools are most likely to break, enabling rigorous validation without requiring access to real patient data.

Realistic Synthetic Clinical Data Generation

An important step in developing safe and robust clinical AI is rigorous validation. However, the data currently available for testing new technologies presents significant challenges.

The Challenge with Existing Data Sources

  • Real Patient Data

    Is sensitive, difficult to access, and lacks a consistent, verifiable 'ground truth' to test an algorithm against.

  • Generative 'Black Box' Models

    Models trained on real data inherit its biases and limitations, and their use still relies on accessing sensitive personal records.

  • Manual Case Studies

    While useful, manually curated vignettes are often unrealistically 'clean' and cannot be produced at the scale needed for robust testing.

My Approach: A Ground-Truth Labeled Data Engine

I am developing a novel engine to automate the generation of large amounts of realistically messy synthetic patient data. Each synthetic record is paired with a high-dimensional set of 'ground-truth' labels, all derived from the highest quality empirical sources of medical knowledge.

This approach creates a safe 'sandbox' to strategically probe for failures in the atypical 'edge cases' where algorithms are most likely to break, enabling rigorous and scalable validation without ever requiring a single piece of real patient data.

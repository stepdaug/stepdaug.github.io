Neurology Reference Sites

A collection of useful, high-quality reference sites for clinical neurology.

  • learningeeg.com

    Educational resource for learning EEG interpretation.

  • epilepsydiagnosis.org

    Educational resource for the diagnosis of epilepsy syndromes.

  • epilepsygenetics.net

    Practical guide to the genetic epilepsies.

  • epilepsypredictiontools.info

    Prediction tools for outcomes in newly diagnosed epilepsy.

  • pdmedcalc.co.uk

    Calculator for Parkinson's disease medication dosages.

  • Myaware Drugs to Avoid

    List of medications to avoid in Myasthenia Gravis.

  • MS Decisions Aid

    Tool to help people make decisions about treatment options in multiple sclerosis.

  • vademetab.org

    Guide to inherited metabolic diseases (Vademecum Metabolicum).
